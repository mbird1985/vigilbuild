The problem

The festival gets a banner, everything else gets a flyer at city hall. Attendance suffers, and residents feel like nothing happens in town.

What VigilBuild does

A real events calendar — month, week, and day views, color-coded by venue, with a detail page for every event. Staff add an event once; residents see it on the website, the app, and their phone. Day view sorts by time, so festival weekends stay organized.

Events, news, and alerts on every screen is how a municipality earns attention back — and attention is what civic engagement is made of.

The problem

Complaints arrive by phone call — and disappear.

A pothole gets reported five times to three different offices. Nobody knows it's the same pothole, nobody closes the loop, and five residents conclude the municipality doesn't listen.

What VigilBuild does

Residents report from their phone with a photo and GPS location. The platform groups duplicate reports into one issue, puts it on the map with a count — “4 residents reported this” — and crews work the biggest problems first. When it's fixed, every reporter is notified automatically.

That closed loop — report → see it worked on → get told it's done — is what turns frustrated callers into engaged residents.

The problem

Paper archives, lost files — and audit season panic.

Residents lose a morning in line to file one form. The form then walks desk to desk, and when the Contraloría asks “who approved this and when?”, the answer is a week of digging through cabinets.

What VigilBuild does

We digitize your documentation end to end. Your exact official forms go online — residents complete and legally e-sign them from a phone, and every approval step happens on screen. Existing paper archives get scanned and loaded into the same secure, searchable database.

Every action writes itself into an immutable audit log — person, timestamp, decision notes, and a digital fingerprint per document. When auditors come, you export the log. The documentation audits ask for now exists as a by-product of daily work.
