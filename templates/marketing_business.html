The problem

Your records live in filing cabinets — until an audit asks for them.

Contracts, inspections, invoices, HR files. Finding one document means someone digging for an afternoon; proving who approved what, when means hoping the right person still works there.

What VigilBuild does

We digitize your documentation end to end: existing paper gets scanned and loaded into a secure database, searchable by name, date, case, or content — and from that day on, new documents are born digital, with electronic signatures, automatic routing between approvers, and email notifications at every step.

Every action is logged immutably — person, timestamp, decision note, and a digital fingerprint per file. Audit season becomes an export button, not a fire drill.
