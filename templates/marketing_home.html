🏛️ Government Management Division

The municipal operating system.

Residents lose mornings in line for one paper. Documents walk from office to office. Federal deadlines pass silently. Our platform ends that — website, citizen app, and back office in one system, in Spanish and English.

📄 Official forms, filed from a phone

Your exact government-approved forms — not recreations — completed and legally e-signed online. The resident gets a stamped copy instantly; your staff sees every step, with a full audit trail for reviewers.

📍 Citizen reports that organize themselves

Photo + GPS reports cluster into one issue per problem, so crews fix what the most residents are reporting first — and every reporter is notified when it's done.

💰 One screen for the money

Finance uploads the Excel sheet it already keeps; the mayor's office sees income and spending charts up to date, with a printable financial report for any period.

🏦 Federal funds with warnings

Every grant with its application and reporting deadlines, awarded and drawn amounts — flagged before anything expires.

🔍 Transparency on autopilot

Budgets, contracts, and reports published from the back office appear on the public Transparency page by themselves — supporting Law 141-2019 obligations.

🌀 Hurricane Mode

The season checklist — before, during, after — with each department's progress on one bar, plus emergency alerts pushed to residents' phones.
